Who makes vISIon

Editorial board

Colleagues from across the ISI's seven associations, bringing different regions, sectors and viewpoints to the table.

The board

Editors

Eighteen editors, listed alphabetically. Between them they commission and shepherd the articles in each issue, read every draft for clarity and accuracy, and represent vISIon within their association.

The magazine is edited by Manuele Leonelli (IE University), Editor-in-Chief. Anything meant for vISIon as a whole, rather than for a particular section, is best sent to manuele.leonelli@ie.edu.

Interested in joining the board?

We are always glad to hear from colleagues who want to help shape the magazine, particularly from regions and associations that are not yet well represented around the table. Board members serve for a renewable term, meet online a few times a year, and take responsibility for a section of the magazine.

Write to us with a line about your background and the part of vISIon you would like to work on.

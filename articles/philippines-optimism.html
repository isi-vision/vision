Country spotlight · October 2025 · Vol. 1 No. 1

Philippines

Mapping optimism in the pearl of the orient seas

Authors

Leonard Allan F. Almero

University of the Philippines Los Baños

Marie Joy L. Relente

University of the Philippines Los Baños

Mara Sherlin DP. Talento

KAUST

In brief

The Philippines is known for its natural beauty and cultural richness, but equally noteworthy is the optimism of its people. Working with the OCTA Research Group's nationwide Tugon ng Masa survey, the authors visualise and analyse that optimism, and along the way demystify how simple survey responses become a statistical index.

“Optimism is less visible than landscapes or landmarks, yet it plays an equally important role in how Filipinos face challenges.”

What the article covers

  • A stratified multi-stage probability sample of 1,200 adults across 240 barangays.
  • 49 per cent of adults expect their quality of life to improve within six months, up 15 points on the previous wave.
  • Moran's I finds no evidence of spatial autocorrelation in regional optimism.
  • How a National Optimism Index could be built properly, and why geographically weighted regression would help.
